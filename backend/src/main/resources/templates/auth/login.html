Features

Everything your movie collection needs

Built for movie lovers who want their favorite films organized, searchable and all in one place.

Your movie library

Add movies with title, director, genre, rating and synopsis, then edit or remove them at any time.

Categories

Group your films into your own categories, such as Action, Drama or Weekend favorites, and browse them easily.

Actors & cast

Keep track of the actors you love and see which movies they appear in, each with their own detail page.

Poster uploads

Upload a poster for every movie so your collection looks like a real cinema wall.

Top rated & search

See your highest-rated movies at a glance and find any title in seconds with search.

Private & secure

Your collection belongs only to you. Nobody else can see or change your entries, and passwords are stored safely encrypted.

About the creator

Made by Armin Jazdanpanahi

Hi, I'm Armin. I built Cinemaland as a full-stack project because I love movies and wanted a simple, good-looking place to keep my own collection. The backend is built with Java 21 and Spring Boot, the frontend with Vue 3, and the data lives in MySQL.
